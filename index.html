Diel
Shift your sleep, night by night

Support

Diel moves your sleep to a new time a few minutes each night, then keeps you there. It reminds you before bedtime and wakes you with an alarm. Free, no account, everything stays on your iPhone.

The wake-up alarm doesn’t ring

Diel’s alarms need permission. Open Settings → Diel and allow Alarms. On the app’s home screen, a banner with “Turn on” appears while permission is missing.

Reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications in Settings → Diel → Notifications. A notification obeys silent mode and Focus; if a reminder must be heard, make it an alarm: in the app, tap Edit on “Reminders tonight”, then tap the reminder’s icon.

Diel asks me to open the app

iOS limits how many reminders and alarms an app can schedule ahead. On long plans, Diel sends one notification a day before the queue runs out. Opening the app is enough to schedule the next weeks.

I fell asleep later than planned

Tap Edit on your plan card and enter when you actually fell asleep and woke up. The plan continues from today; nights already lived stay as they were.

I want a different target time

Tap Edit on your plan card and change the target or sleep length. Saving starts a new protocol from where you are today. If you change your mind, you can undo it on the home screen for a day.

Turning everything off for a day

Tap Edit on “Reminders tonight” and switch off the wake-up alarm or any reminder. Remember to switch them back on.
